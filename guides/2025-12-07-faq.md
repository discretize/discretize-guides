# Frequently Asked Questions
 
<Card title="Is the website up-to-date?">
The website is currently not actively being maintained. However, the fractal meta is incredibly stable. The featured builds are very likely still the best in the game mode. There might be new relics released or balance changes that slightly alter portrayed builds.    
</Card>

<Card title="Where can I find additional information?">
For active game discussions, check out the [dT Discord](https://discord.com/invite/Kscq6qda). There you can find guides for new fractal releases, discuss the impact of balance changes, find videos of new specs and nuanced information on boss specific guides.
</Card>

<Card title="What's the current fractal meta?">
[The dT comp](https://www.discretize.eu/guides/daily-comps/) displays a relaxed daily meta comp that has withstood the test of time. Certain fractals with longer phases like Kinfall, Sunqua Peak or Silent Surf can favour specific compositions that are much more subject to current (raid) balance. Check [gw2wingman](https://gw2wingman.nevermindcreations.de/kinfall) if you want to get an idea of the current top specs for each fractal and current patch record clear times. 
</Card>


<Card title="Which new specs did Visions of Eternity introduce for fractals?">
Particularly notable specs include quickness ritualist and alacrity amalgam with great damage and utility. On the more raid like fractals (Kinfall, Sunqua Peak or Silent Surf), evoker and conduit perform amazingly, as well. Antiquary introduced a new potent thief spec with both condition and power builds. However, future long-term performance will be subject to balance patches in December and January. 
</Card>

<Card title="Is Chrono the best healer?">
Yes. It simply is the most flexible and versatile healer. It makes comp building easy and handles mechanics well. Other healers can have situational upsides. For example, Troubadour can group distort the spread mechanic on Sunqua Peak and Paragon can skip the add explosion mechanic alltogether with its elite skill. 
</Card>

<Card title="Can I play specs that are not featured on the website?">
Anything is good enough to get you through fractals. Some things are smarter than others. Play what you want.\
As mentioned, [gw2wingman](https://gw2wingman.nevermindcreations.de/kinfall) and [dT Discord](https://discord.com/invite/Kscq6qda) can give you more accurate, up-to-date information. 
</Card>

<Card title="How do I adjust a raid build for fractals?">
Make sure your build runs <Item id="24868"/> or specific slaying sigils and potions according to the [cheat sheet](https://www.discretize.eu/guides/cheat-sheet/). \
Don't be afraid to adjust utility skills to help out your team in specific situations, as well.
</Card>

<Card title="Are flux bomb and afflicted instability punishments from God?">
Probably.
</Card>
