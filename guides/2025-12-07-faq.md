# Frequently Asked Questions
   
<Card title="Is the website up-to-date?">
The website is currently not actively being maintained. However, the fractal meta is incredibly stable. The featured builds are very likely still the best in the game mode. There might be new relics released or balance changes that slightly alter portrayed builds. Situationally, other builds might also perform great.  
</Card>

<Card title="Where can I find additional information?">
For active game discussions, check out the [dT Discord](https://discord.com/invite/Kscq6qda). There you can find guides for new fractal releases, discuss the impact of balance changes, find videos of new specs and nuanced information on boss specific guides.
</Card>

<Card title="What's the current fractal meta?">
[The dT daily comp](https://www.discretize.eu/guides/daily-comps/) displays a relaxed daily meta comp that has withstood the test of time. Certain fractals with longer phases like Kinfall, Sunqua Peak or Silent Surf can favour specific compositions that are much more subject to current (raid) balance. Check [gw2wingman](https://gw2wingman.nevermindcreations.de/kinfall) if you want to get an idea of the current top specs for each fractal and current patch record clear times. 
</Card>


<Card title="Which new specs did Visions of Eternity introduce for fractals?">
Particularly notable specs with great damage and utility include quickness ritualist and alacrity amalgam. On the more raid like fractals (Kinfall, Sunqua Peak or Silent Surf), evoker and conduit perform amazingly, as well. Antiquary introduced a new potent thief spec with both condition and power builds. However, future long-term performance will be subject to balance patches in January 2026 and thereafter. 
</Card>

<Card title="Any other notable specs missing from the website?">
Power Bladesworn and Power Weaver are two fractals classics that can be good, as well, if you know what you are doing, especially in groups which do not pre-trap bosses. If you are a thief enjoyer, ask your local spec dealer about the current state of cele heal spectre.   
</Card>

<Card title="Is Chrono the best healer?">
Yes. It simply is the most flexible and versatile healer. It makes comp building easy and handles mechanics well. However, other healers can have situational upsides. For example, Troubadour can group distort the spread mechanic on Sunqua Peak and Paragon can skip the add explosion mechanic alltogether with its elite skill. Any good healer in fractals should be able to strip boons, bring a lot of cc, aegis and stability. Preventing damage is better than healing up after the fact.  
</Card>

<Card title="Can I play specs that are not featured on the website?">
Anything is good enough to get you through fractals. Some things are smarter than others. Play what you want.\
As mentioned, [gw2wingman](https://gw2wingman.nevermindcreations.de/kinfall) and [dT Discord](https://discord.com/invite/Kscq6qda) can give you more accurate, up-to-date information. 
</Card>

<Card title="How do I adjust a raid build for fractals?">
Make sure your power build runs <Item id="24868"/> or specific slaying sigils and potions according to the [cheat sheet](https://www.discretize.eu/guides/cheat-sheet/). \
Don't be afraid to adjust utility skills to help out your team in specific situations, as well. \
Condition builds are usually simply raid build copies, potentially with lower boon duration due to precasts. 
</Card>
